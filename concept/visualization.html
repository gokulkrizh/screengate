⚠️ Technical Implementation

ScreenDiet uses iOS DeviceActivityMonitor with threshold-based tracking. Here's how it works:

📊 20-Monitor Allocation

• 🍩 CARBS: 8 monitors (3-min threshold, 9.6 hrs)
• 🥑 FATS: 6 monitors (5-min threshold, 12 hrs)
• 🥩 PROTEIN: 4 monitors (5-min threshold, 8 hrs)
• 🥦 FIBER: 2 monitors (5-min threshold, 4 hrs)

⏱️ Tracking Accuracy

• CARBS: ±3 minutes (3-min thresholds)
• Other categories: ±5 minutes
• Usage <3 min may not count
• Resets daily at midnight

🚫 iOS Limitations

• No individual app breakdown
• 20 monitors = iOS maximum
• Extension runs independently
• Privacy sandboxed data

🔧 How Threshold Tracking Works

Each monitor fires events every 3-5 minutes of usage. Extension counts events and stores in shared UserDefaults. Main app reads totals and displays "~45 min" with ±3 min accuracy.

Reference: Apple DeviceActivityMonitor Docs
